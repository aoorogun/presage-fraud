# Fairness note

The simulated world contains no demographic attributes and none were invented. A demographic fairness analysis is therefore NOT performed. `reports/tables/subgroup_by_bank.csv` compares false-positive rate, recall and intervention rate across the five simulated institutions; this is an operational subgroup check, not a fairness audit. Any deployment must evaluate error-rate parity on legitimately held protected or proxy attributes under the operator's legal basis.
